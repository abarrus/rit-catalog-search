MECE-300

Intermediate Machining and Fabrication Lab

1 credits

This hands-on laboratory will instruct students in the safe operation of CNC and manual milling, turning, drilling, and other machine shop equipment. Students will develop fundamental machine shop skills needed to function in a machine shop environment. Students will learn to interpret drawings to layout, setup, and machine a series of parts used to build working prototypes. Project may require approximately 2 hours of additional out of class project work in the machine shop each week.This course may be used towards a free elective or engineering topic credit.
