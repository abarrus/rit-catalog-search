PHPS-563

Forensic Photography

3 credits

Proper documentation of crime scenes and evidence is extremely important in the investigation of crimes by police agencies and forensic labs. This course will provide hands-on experience documenting crime scenes and related evidence. Students will learn how to prepare images for presentation in court. Topics covered will include crime scene management, evidence handling, crime scene documentation, general evidence documentation, photographic techniques for the enhancement of evidence, and court display preparation. This is a blended course, with lectures delivered online and two in-person Saturday lab sessions.

Pre-requisites: PHPS-102 or PHAR-102 or PHAR-161 or equivalent course.
