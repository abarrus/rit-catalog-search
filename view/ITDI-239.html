ITDI-239

Figure Painting

3 credits

This class is structured to explore materials and techniques in order to paint the human form. Theory and practice of color and drawing, as well as other resources, will be used to develop an understanding of how to portray the figure. Traditional and contemporary approaches to figurative painting are utilized in producing figure paintings. **Fee: A materials fee is required for this course, and an additional course fee applied via student account**
