CRIM-350

Criminology

3 credits

This course provides an in-depth exploration of the field of criminology, focusing on the causes, consequences, and societal responses to crime. Students will analyze key criminological theories, patterns of criminal behavior, and the role of the criminal justice system in addressing crime. The course emphasizes the connections among the world’s population by exploring global crime phenomena, highlighting how crime transcends national borders. Students will also examine similarities and differences in human experiences, investigating how cultural, historical, and societal contexts shape varying perspectives on crime, punishment, and rehabilitation. Through critical thinking and evidence-based approaches, this course fosters an understanding of the ethical dimensions of crime and justice while providing a foundation for further studies in criminology and related disciplines.
